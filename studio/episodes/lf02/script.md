## CLAIMS -> SOURCES (According to PubMed; DOI links)
1. Stress -> brain -> adrenal glands -> adrenaline & cortisol; heart rate, breathing, glucose up (fight-or-flight) — standard physiology textbook.
2. Long-running stress -> glucocorticoid receptor resistance -> immune cells don't down-regulate inflammation; higher cold risk — Cohen et al., PNAS 2012 (Carnegie Mellon). https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1118355109
3. Viral challenge, 394 people; infection ~74% -> ~90% and clinical colds ~27% -> 47% with rising stress — Cohen, Tyrrell & Smith, NEJM 1991. https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM199108293250903
4. Holiday-season cardiac events peak around Christmas & New Year in some data; hypotheses = overeating, salt, alcohol, fireplace particulates, delayed care, holiday anxiety — Kloner, Prog Cardiovasc Dis 2006. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pcad.2005.07.001  Counter-evidence (say "not every study"): Milne 2005 (spike New Year not Christmas, NE England) https://doi.org/10.1007/s10654-005-2147-8 ; Weerasinghe 2002 (no increase, NSW) https://doi.org/10.1136/heart.88.1.30
5. 12-week slow-breathing RCT (n=100): anxiety (PROMIS) fell; exhale>inhale vs equal ratio not significantly different — Birdee et al., Complement Ther Med 2023. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ctim.2023.102937
6. Physical activity vs mindfulness meditation vs HRV-biofeedback slow breathing, 5 weeks: all reduced stress, no significant difference — van der Zwan et al., Appl Psychophysiol Biofeedback 2015. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10484-015-9293-x
