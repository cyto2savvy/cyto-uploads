## Claims / sources
1. Burk, Mayer, Wiese, Physiol Behav 2019: ~2000 salivary testosterone samples from 82 football fans (53% men) watching 2014 World Cup matches of their favourite national team; testosterone rose most during experiences of challenge (close/competitive phases); returned to pre-contest baseline once a win became certain; moderated by how important the match was. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physbeh.2019.112596
2. Casto et al., Hum Nat 2021: 26 male combat athletes; salivary T before/after watching a teammate compete; T reactivity varied; stronger T reactivity in those for whom team membership was more central to identity, esp. if teammate won. Authors: EXPLORATORY, small sample, interpret with caution. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12110-021-09407-7
