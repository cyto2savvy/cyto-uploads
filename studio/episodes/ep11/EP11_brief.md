## Claims / sources (every claim traced; links go on screen, in description and pinned comment)
1. Orr et al., Physiol Behav 1997: 10 healthy men per experiment; solid meal shortened post-meal sleep latency vs equal volume of water; high-fat vs high-carb vs mixed meals showed no significant difference. https://doi.org/10.1016/s0031-9384(97)00012-7
2. Lehrskov et al., Mol Metab 2018: double-blind crossover RCT, 8 lean + 8 obese men; blocking IL-1 (anakinra) reduced post-meal fatigue after a high-fat high-carb meal (Stanford Sleepiness Scale), more in obese. 'IL-1 is involved', authors say clinical relevance unproven. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molmet.2018.04.001
3. Caveats (pinned comment): both studies small, men only; 1997 study measured nap sleep latency, not 'food coma' feeling; do NOT say 'IL-1 causes' the coma. No claim about turkey/tryptophan.
Attribution line for description: "Sources via PubMed." + the DOI links above. Add "Not medical advice" line.
