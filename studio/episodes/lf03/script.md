## CLAIMS -> SOURCES
1. Salivary amylase begins starch digestion; large fatty meals empty from the stomach more slowly; carbs -> glucose -> insulin moves glucose into cells — standard physiology textbook.
2. Carb-rich vs protein-rich breakfast: tryptophan:LNAA ratio higher after carbs (median difference 54%), insulin rose after carbs not protein; protein meal lowers the ratio — Wurtman et al., Am J Clin Nutr 2003 (MIT). https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/77.1.128  Supporting: Ashley et al., J Neural Transm 1985 (carb meal raised, 20% protein meal lowered the ratio). https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01252031  NOTE: sleepiness not measured, script says so.
3. Exercise AFTER a meal reduced postprandial glucose vs before-meal exercise and vs no exercise; shorter meal-to-exercise gap = bigger effect — Engeroff et al., Sports Med 2023 (meta-analysis). https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01808-7
4. Light walking breaks reduced postprandial glucose and insulin vs prolonged sitting — Buffey et al., Sports Med 2022 (meta-analysis). https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01649-4
5. Heavy meal among triggers with the highest individual odds ratios for MI (population share 2.7%) — Nawrot et al., Lancet 2011. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(10)62296-9
6. Winter-holiday weight and visceral-fat increase persisted at one year (Romania, n=120) — Udroiu et al., Medicina 2026. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina62030511
7. College students gained ~0.74 kg over the holidays, mostly lean mass, body fat % unchanged — Yoo et al., Obes Sci Pract 2025. https://doi.org/10.1002/osp4.70035
8. "Call 911 for chest pain" = standard safety advice. Eglinton Crosstown (Line 5) opened Feb 8 2026 after ~15 years (CTV/CP24) — joke only, optional on-screen.
