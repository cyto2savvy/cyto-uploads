# Ep 17 claims and sources: "Why Quitting Smoking Is So Hard (And What Helps)"
Read from PubMed records (PMIDs 42409118, 43000578) on Nov 11, 2026. Not in sources_used.md (`check_sources.py` = OK).

| # | Line in the video | Claim, exactly as supported | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Nicotine locks onto brain receptors, so cravings hit..." | Cytisine is a partial agonist of nicotinic acetylcholine receptors that reduces withdrawal and attenuates the rewarding effects of smoking (the receptors nicotine acts on) | Diana 2026, Pulm Ther, doi 10.1007/s41030-026-00402-w, PMID 43000578 |
| 2 | "Italian lung screening program" | RISP lung cancer screening trial, 10,191 enrolled, 7,805 with current smoking history; 3,548 pursued a cessation program | Cani 2026, J Thorac Oncol, doi 10.1016/j.jtho.2026.104071, PMID 42409118 |
| 3 | "half of smokers who chose ... cytisine quit" | Cessation 50.1% among cytisine users (n=1,375) vs 8.1% with counselling alone (n=2,173); aOR 14.53 (11.67-18.08). Primary endpoint: self-reported continuous abstinence in the 2 weeks after the program | same |
| 4 | "they picked it themselves, so it's not a fair race" | Cytisine was optional, not randomized; uptake was linked to heavier smoking AND higher motivation (p<0.05). Short-term, self-reported outcome | same |
| 5 | "ask your doctor or pharmacist" | WHO recommends cytisine alongside varenicline, NRT and bupropion; added to WHO Essential Medicines in 2025. Availability differs by country | Diana 2026 |

## Caveats on screen / in description
- NOT a randomized trial; self-selected, short follow-up, self-reported (stated in the script and description).
- Side effects: nausea 36.2%, insomnia 19.0%, abdominal discomfort 18.1% (description).
- The video does not say cytisine is available or approved in Canada. Description says availability differs by country, ask a doctor or pharmacist.
- "Not medical advice" spoken + description + pinned comment.
