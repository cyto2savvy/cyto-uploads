## Caveats that MUST appear in the description or pinned comment
- Masturbation frequency alone: OR 1.12 (0.82-1.54), NOT significant (Raeisvandi). Intercourse frequency, partner count, age at first intercourse: all non-significant in the same meta-analysis.
- Firsty 2025 (systematic review, 10 studies, 6,601 cases): intercourse 5-12+ times a month linked to lower risk; very frequent masturbation (2-7+/week) showed a POSSIBLE increase; more lifetime partners linked to higher risk. In the description so we are not cherry-picking.
- The video says nothing about a month of abstinence being good or bad. No claim about testosterone, "superpowers" or sperm quality.
- "Not medical advice" is spoken and in the description and pinned comment.
