# Why views stall near 1k (data pulled Nov 9, 2026 via YouTube Analytics API through Zapier; 11 videos, 22 subs, 6.4k total views)
- 85% of views come from the Shorts feed (5.4k of 6.3k in 14 days); search is 2.5%. Growth = Shorts feed test batches.
- Results are bimodal: hits cluster at 1.25k-1.48k (Eps 2, 3, 5, 9), everything else is 67-392. Nothing has been pushed past the first test batch.
- Retention: hits keep ~90% of viewers through the first 20% of the video; Ep 7 (67 views) fell to 59% by 20% and 38% by 24%. Every video has a cliff at 15-25% (~5-7s) = right after the hook.
- Subs: ~0.35% of views convert (22 subs). Ep 1 (looped, 93% avg viewed) shows the loop works.
- Duds were time-locked/niche (clocks, hockey) or vague (procrastination); hits were universal myth/"can you actually" body questions.
