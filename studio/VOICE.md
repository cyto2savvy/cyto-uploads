# Cyto's voice — Toronto lingo + references (read before writing ANY script)

Cyto talks like a chill Toronto youth explaining science to his boys. Never professional, never a lecture.
The science stays exact; only the delivery is Toronto.

## The rules
- **Every 30s script needs:** at least **3 Toronto slang words** and **1 Toronto reference** (place, transit, sports, weather, food, event). The catchphrase doesn't count.
- **Context makes it clear.** A viewer in Ohio should still get the sentence. One slang word per sentence, max two.
- **Body part = Toronto place** (use the map below so it stays consistent across episodes).
- **Slang words get caption colour** like science keywords do, so they read as the brand.
- **Never:** slurs; slang about women ("ting" for a girl, "gyal"); drug, drinking or getting-high slang (cup rule); crime/violence slang ("opp", "smoke", "ching"); real brand names; real people's fake quotes; politics.
- Don't stack every word into one line ("wagwan mandem, ahlie bare styll fam") — that's cringe. Natural > dense.

## Delivery (how Cyto SOUNDS — Utsha records it himself from Nov 2026)
Reference: "Story Time with Bucks" (BlockTalk, youtube.com/shorts/KDzzcQ_ZP-0) — a Toronto mans telling a story to his boys. **Borrow the cadence, never the content, lines or his actual voice** (no cloning a real person).
- **Story-time energy, not a lecture.** Talk like you're telling the mandem what happened: "So boom —", "bro said…", "and I'm like…". Science = the story.
- **Present tense for the action:** "Cortisol pulls up at 3am and starts banging on the door."
- **Tags at the end of lines, said quick and low:** "…ahlie." "…styll." "…you dun know." Don't punch them; throw them away.
- **Drag the reaction words:** "bruuuh", "naaah", "it's bare deep, styll."
- **Laugh at your own line sometimes.** A small laugh before the punchline reads as real.
- **Pauses before the twist:** "And the worst part? … (beat) … it's your own hormones."
- **Speed:** fast through setup, slow down on the one fact that matters.
- **From the Bucks transcript (cadence only — his story is about violence, never use content):** opens cold with "Yo, one time I was…"; one long run-on chained with "and… and…"; flat, understated reactions ("after that I can't even relax"); "**though**" thrown on the end of a line as a tag ("I thought I was gonna die though"). Cyto version: "So boom, one time it's 3am…", "Can't even relax after that, though."
- **Never forced.** If a slang word doesn't come out natural in your mouth, swap it.

**Record:** phone voice memo, quiet room, a hand's length from the mic, ~20% more hype than normal (the disguise flattens energy). One take per line is fine; I cut them together.

## Toronto references (no brand names)
- **Transit:** TTC delays, Line 1 shuttle buses, streetcar short-turns, Union Station at rush hour, the 401 / DVP / Gardiner traffic and construction.
- **Places:** CN Tower, Kensington Market, the Bluffs, Toronto Island ferry lineup, High Park cherry blossoms, Yonge-Dundas, Nathan Phillips Square skating rink, the PATH.
- **Sports:** Leafs playoff pain, Raptors 2019, Jays, TFC (no team logos on screen; team colours are fine).
- **Weather:** minus 20 with the windchill, slush puddles at the curb, the one week of spring, humid August.
- **Food:** patties, roti, shawarma, a double-double, food from the ends.
- **Events:** Caribana, the Ex (CNE), Nuit Blanche, Pride, Diwali/Lunar New Year/Eid in the city, Santa Claus Parade.
- **City life:** raccoons winning, condo cranes everywhere, rent prices, 416 vs 905.

## TTS pronunciation (ElevenLabs / any text-to-speech) — added Nov 2, 2026
Captions stay as written (they come from `segs.json`); only the text SENT to the voice is respelled.
| Caption word | Send to TTS | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| ahlie | `ah-lie` (stress the "lie": "ah-LIE") | rhymes with "pie", not "ally" |
| styll | `stiiill` (3 i's, TESTED + APPROVED Nov 2, voice "Cyto - Toronto (A)"); fallbacks `stilllllll`, `sti-iiill` | long, dragged "stiill". Avoid `steeyull` and 4+ i's |
Rules: respell per take and listen once; if a respelling reads wrong, move to the next fallback and record which one won here. Elongated spellings can add a stray "ee" sound, so keep the vowel run short (3-4 letters). Put the word at the END of a sentence/phrase so the drag has room (tags like "styll." work best last).
