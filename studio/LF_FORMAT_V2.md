# Long-form FORMAT v2 (from LF01 feedback: "feels like an AI bot explaining an infographic")
Applies to LF02 (Dec 18) and LF03 (Dec 25) and every long video after.

THE RULE: it's a cartoon EPISODE with a plot, not a narrated slideshow. Science is revealed THROUGH the story.
1. Plot: a goal + a ticking clock + a villain/obstacle (LF02: stop the stress alarm before Christmas while Rhino sneaks in; LF03: survive one giant dinner, put the turkey on trial).
2. Cast talks TO EACH OTHER. Cyto + Neutro (deadpan) + Mito (hype then crash). Rhino the cold virus never speaks (mime + SFX). Banter > lecture.
3. Cold open = mid-chaos in the first 3 seconds (siren / delivery truck), a line of dialogue, title slam by 0:12.
4. A "beat" every 20-30 s: joke, twist, reveal, sight gag or "bro WHAT" moment. No 30 s stretch without a laugh or a surprise.
5. Diagrams are seasoning: max ~1 per minute, max ~4 s each, always with a character reacting to it. Locations (Toronto-body sets) carry the explaining.
6. Bits that hit: courtroom, heist sneak, video-game "CHEAT CODE UNLOCKED" / "SIDE QUEST COMPLETE" pop-ups, Mannequin-Challenge freeze ("2026 is the new 2016" nostalgia trend), countdown clock, energy bar, callbacks across parts.
7. Two-part special: Part 1 ends on a cliffhanger into Part 2; Part 2 calls back to Part 1. Pinned comment links the other part.
8. Comedy timing in animation: hold 0.4-0.6 s on reaction faces before the punchline; snap zoom + SFX on punchlines; quiet beat (silence) before a "bro WHAT".
9. Real Toronto Dec 2026 refs only if verified: Distillery Winter Village (Nov 12 2026-Jan 3 2027, free before 4 pm), Nathan Phillips Square skating/lights, Line 1 rush hour, the Gardiner, Union Station, Eglinton Crosstown finally open (Feb 2026). No real logos/brands/people.
10. Slang: Toronto + light Gen Z (lowkey, fam, bro, ahlie, styll, side quest, sus, cooked, "I'm him"). Never forced, never more than a couple per minute.
Voices: one ElevenLabs take per CHARACTER containing all that character's lines in order, separated by a pause; the build session cuts lines with Scribe word timings and sequences them per the script.
